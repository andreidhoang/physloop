D7 note (not legal advice):
- Law 36/2024/QH15 (Road Traffic Order and Safety), effective 2025-01-01.
  - Original Art. 34(4): smart vehicle = "xe cơ giới cho phép tự động hóa một phần hoặc toàn bộ hoạt động điều khiển phương tiện, xác định lộ trình và xử lý tình huống" (partial or full automation). Read from an unofficial apolatlegal copy.
  - Law 118/2025/QH15, effective 2026-07-01, replaced this with: a road vehicle "mà toàn bộ việc điều khiển, duy trì hoạt động của phương tiện không cần có sự điều khiển trực tiếp của người lái" (fully driverless). Read from the official consolidated VBHN 55/VBHN-VPQH.
  - Art. 35(3): smart vehicles that are motor vehicles or special-use vehicles must meet the registration and technical conditions of 35(1) and be permitted to operate by a competent authority. The Government details this (35(5)).
- Decree 151/2024/NĐ-CP, Art. 25 (read from mirrors):
  - the Traffic Police Department (Cục CSGT, Ministry of Public Security) issues the permit;
  - "cơ quan, tổ chức, cá nhân là chủ sở hữu" may apply, so individuals are not excluded;
  - the file is an application form plus a technical safety certificate.
  - Decree 236/2026/NĐ-CP (effective 2026-07-01) amends Decree 151; no change to the smart-vehicle rules surfaced.
- Law 35/2024/QH15 (Roads, effective 2025-01-01) has no vehicle-level AV rule.
  - Art. 40 "Giao thông thông minh" covers ITS infrastructure.
  - "tự lái" in Art. 78 means self-drive car rental, a false friend.
- Dashcams:
  - No mandatory rule and no prohibition found for private cars.
  - Art. 35(2) requires journey monitors and driver-image cameras only for commercial and specified vehicles.
  - Law 91/2025/QH15 on personal data protection, Art. 32 (effective 2026-01-01; read from a provincial government page) allows recording in public without consent in listed cases, but requires notice that recording is happening.
- Uncertain:
  - whether a passive camera rig with no actuation is a "phương tiện giao thông thông minh" under either version of the definition (on the plain wording it seems not to be, but that is a question for counsel);
  - the đối tượng áp dụng text of HCMC Res. 20/2024;
  - the Hanoi decision number;
  - whether Decree 151 Art. 25 was re-issued after Law 118/2025.
