Only a small core is about end-to-end/VLA driving models, closed-loop RL or AV evaluation: Role A (JR2010863), the AV Research pair (JR2005596 and JR2005597), the deep-RL role (JR2022179), the end-to-end model integration role (JR2025038), the metrics-and-evaluation role (JR2014651) and the China-based end-to-end role (JR2019758). ### Inferences
- In NVIDIA's own language, "Principal" means demonstrated cross-team technical authority (vision, architecture, roadmap, standards, mentoring), backed by 12–15+ years. Nothing in the postings requires a PhD or publications at Principal level, except the research-scientist variant, where publications and OSS libraries are required.
- The checkable public substitutes that postings themselves name are: "technical direction" and "architectural decisions" (design docs others adopt), "software libraries used by a broad developer community", publication records, and "taking models from prototype to production". This supports 03 §0's "Principal, stripped of tenure" reading, but the tenure lines are explicit requirements and cannot be waived by the text.
- The posting "Level n" numbering appears to coincide with levels.fyi's "ICn" numbering (Senior titles at L3–L5, Principal at L6). This is an inference: NVIDIA publishes no mapping.

### Gaps
- No official NVIDIA level-to-title map was found. levels.fyi and Blind data are self-reported (secondary).
- Postings give base pay only. Equity, which dominates NVIDIA total compensation per levels.fyi, is not quantified in postings.

## Q4. Which skills recur across comparable competitor and NVIDIA postings, and which are "required" versus "preferred"?

### Takeaway
Across 255 comparable senior-to-principal postings (43 NVIDIA plus 212 from 14 competitors, all read from primary job-board feeds), the most common qualifications are generic engineering ones: Python (65% NVIDIA, 64% competitors), C++ (67%, 58%; nearly always "required"), ML frameworks, evaluation/metrics, and production delivery. VLA/VLM/LLM/foundation-model experience is the leading AI-specific cluster (49% of NVIDIA, 27% of competitor qualifications), split roughly evenly between required and preferred. RL, world-model/generative, CUDA, publications and open source are mostly "preferred". "Closed-loop" appears in role descriptions but almost never as a qualification line.

### Inferences
- Demand maps onto the 03 skill pillars as follows:
  - **P1, driving as a decision problem:** planning/behavior/prediction is in about 30% of qualifications at both NVIDIA and competitors. Planning-heavy roles (NVIDIA JR2024273, Kodiak, Motional, Zoox) gate on C++ plus classical planning, with learned planning as a growing addition.
  - **P2, VLA modeling:** the largest AI-specific demand. 49% of NVIDIA and 27% of competitor qualifications mention VLA/VLM/LLM/foundation models. It is required in about half of those and preferred in the rest.
  - **P3, learning algorithms:** RL appears in 23% (NVIDIA) and 16% (competitors) of qualifications; generative/world-model methods in 21% and 13%. Flow matching and diffusion are named explicitly (NVIDIA JR2022179, Waymo, Nuro).
  - **P4, data engine:** 23% and 17%. Dedicated flywheel/mining roles exist: Waymo "Staff MLE, Data Flywheel", Waymo "Senior Staff TLM, Data Mining and Sampling for ML and Evaluation", Motional "Principal MLE, Data Mining".
  - **P5, simulation and evaluation:** evaluation is the most common responsibility (51% NVIDIA, 57% competitors) and a frequent qualification (28%, 39%). The strict phrase "closed-loop" rarely gates hiring, so closed-loop evaluation is mostly work to be done, not a credential screen. Statistical validation (confidence intervals, p-values, precision/recall of metrics) appears explicitly at Zoox and NVIDIA.
  - **P6, deployment and real-time:** C++ is the most common hard requirement. CUDA (40% of NVIDIA qualifications) and real-time/embedded (35%) are more NVIDIA-heavy than at competitors. Quantization/TensorRT is concentrated in dedicated inference roles (Zoox inference, NVIDIA JR2025038/JR2016768, XPeng LLM Quantization & Deployment).
  - **P7, safety and production:** safety/V&V standards appear in about 19% of qualifications at both. "Shipped production" (narrow phrasing) appears in 24–28%, mostly as required.
- Within this sample, NVIDIA postings over-index on VLA/LLM, CUDA, real-time/embedded, open source and imitation/end-to-end. Competitors over-index on evaluation, distributed training and quantization. Competitor quantization demand comes from specialized inference roles.
- Staff/Principal titles show only modest shifts toward publications, RL, evaluation and safety. Seniority is signalled mainly by leadership, scope and years, not by a different technical skill set.
