No NVIDIA closed-loop result for the AlpaGym expert update was found, so D-023 records the trained arm as a shipped recipe at registered settings.

## Strata and guard pricing (agent-authored)

Priced the S4 improvement claim through the locked gate with a design runner that scores every design on the same simulated rows, checked against exact count enumeration (`evidence/strata-pricing-20261108-v1/`, note in `docs/research/2026-11-08-principal-review/strata-pricing.md`).
Requiring improvement in every stratum has no power beyond one stratum (0.000 for four 110-road strata even at 5 pp). Pooled improvement keeps 0.85 at 3 pp with b = 0 but only 0.09 at b = 2%. A 0.10 per-stratum guard stops a pooled claim when a 41-road stratum is harmed by 10 pp (0.333 to 0.016), but a 0.05 guard blocks harmless claims. Three replicates lowered power from 0.097 to 0.020 at b = 2% because the bound ignores variance.
Next hypothesis: paired discordance b on the registered path, with and without seed coupling, decides whether any S4 improvement claim is reachable, so S2 should measure it before training is bought; nothing is registered until Huy records the design.
Correction after independent review: the first guard-sizing table was per stratum, but the guard needs every gating stratum GO together. Four 110-road strata at b = 2% pass jointly 0.495, not 0.84; two gating strata need about 120 roads each and four about 150. Guarding a 41-road stratum protects it against a 10 pp harm (0.333 to 0.016) at about half the power (0.451 to 0.239), while reporting it instead keeps 0.437. Rule v4 (registered guard and gating strata, paired-observation and comparability checks) passes 94 tests with 29 of 30 mutants killed (`evidence/strata-pricing-20261108-v2/`).
